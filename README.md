## 🌸 MY LEARNING JOURNEY 🌸

*More than just completing activities*

</div>

Throughout GE 4120, I had the opportunity to explore different digital applications and discover how they can be used to turn ideas into actual outputs. Each activity introduced me to a different aspect of technology, from designing visual materials and organizing data to generating images using AI and developing a business website.

One of the things I appreciated most was learning that these tools are not limited to one purpose. They can be used to communicate ideas, solve problems, organize information, and make certain tasks more manageable.

These experiences also taught me the importance of creativity, patience, critical thinking, and responsible use of technology. I know there is still more to learn, but this portfolio represents the progress I have made and the skills I can continue developing.
