# Activity 6: Making a Website – Replit Implementation

## Short Description

<p align="justify">
This activity focused on developing a one-page business website using Replit AI. The chosen business concept was Peonydia Cases, a proposed phone case brand offering floral, cute and kawaii, minimalist, and personalized phone case designs. Using a detailed prompt, the AI generated a website that presented the brand identity, product collections, featured products, About Us section, brand highlights, and contact and order inquiry form. The project also involved documenting the website development process through screenshots of the prompts, website sections, and its live link.
</p>

## What Have I Learned?

<p align="justify">
Developing the Peonydia Cases website helped me understand how a business idea can be presented through an actual online platform. I learned that a good website needs more than an attractive appearance; it should also have organized content, clear navigation, consistent branding, and useful features that make it easier for visitors to explore the products. One challenge was translating the business concept into detailed instructions that Replit AI could follow, especially when specifying the website sections, design preferences, and expected functions. I addressed this by preparing a more specific prompt and reviewing the generated website to see if the different sections matched my intended concept. I also realized that AI can make website creation more accessible, but human review is still necessary to check the quality and functionality of the final output. In future projects, I can use this experience to develop websites for other business ideas and improve my ability to plan and communicate technical requirements.
</p>
