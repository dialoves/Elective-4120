# Activity 4: Google Sheets

## Short Description

<p align="justify">
This activity focused on understanding and exploring Google Sheets as a spreadsheet application used for organizing data, performing calculations, and managing information. It included a group presentation discussing its purpose, functions, features, common formulas, and practical applications in school, business, personal budgeting, healthcare, research, and travel planning. The presentation also featured an interactive activity called "Budget or Burado?" where participants applied basic computation and Google Sheets formulas to determine whether expenses were within or over a given budget.
</p>

## What Have I Learned?

<p align="justify">
Through this activity, I learned that Google Sheets is more than just a tool for encoding information because it can also perform calculations, organize data, and make information easier to understand. I became more familiar with formulas such as SUM, AVERAGE, COUNT, IF, MIN, MAX, and RANK, and understood how they can be used for different tasks. The examples and interactive Budget or Burado activity helped me see how these formulas can be applied to everyday situations, such as tracking expenses, managing budgets, and organizing school activities. I also learned about useful features such as real-time collaboration, automatic saving, sharing permissions, and version history. As a nursing student, I can apply this knowledge when organizing research data, preparing reports, and managing information while observing proper data privacy and confidentiality.
</p>
