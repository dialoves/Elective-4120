# Activity 4: Google Sheets

## Short Description

This activity focused on understanding and exploring Google Sheets as a spreadsheet application used for organizing data, performing calculations, and managing information. It included a group presentation discussing its purpose, functions, features, common formulas, and practical applications in school, business, personal budgeting, healthcare, research, and travel planning. The presentation also featured an interactive activity called "Budget or Burado?" where participants applied Google Sheets formulas and basic computation to determine whether expenses were within or over a given budget.

## What Have I Learned?

Through this activity, I learned that Google Sheets is more than just a tool for encoding information because it can also perform calculations, organize data, and make information easier to understand. I became more familiar with formulas such as SUM, AVERAGE, COUNT, IF, MIN, MAX, and RANK, and understood how they can be used to solve different tasks automatically. One important thing I learned was how these functions can be applied to everyday situations, such as tracking expenses, managing budgets, monitoring school activities, and organizing healthcare information. Understanding the different formulas and knowing when to use them required practice, which became easier through the examples and interactive Budget or Burado activity. As a nursing student, I can apply this knowledge in organizing patient-related data, preparing reports, and managing research information while maintaining proper confidentiality.
