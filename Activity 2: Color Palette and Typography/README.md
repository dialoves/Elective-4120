# Activity 2: Personal Branding

## Short Description

<p align="justify">
This activity focused on developing a personal brand identity by creating a logo, color palette, typography set, and branding materials for a flower studio named Peonydia. The design featured a floral-inspired concept with soft and elegant colors, complemented by carefully selected fonts to establish a consistent and recognizable brand identity. It also included a brand contact page presenting the studio's name, social media account, and contact information.
</p>

## What Have I Learned?

<p align="justify">
Through this activity, I learned how important consistency is when creating a brand identity. I gained experience in combining different visual elements, such as logos, colors, and typography, to communicate a particular style and personality. One challenge was selecting colors and fonts that complemented each other while maintaining the soft and elegant floral concept of Peonydia. I addressed this by developing a coordinated color palette and choosing typography with different purposes for headings, body text, and accents. This activity helped me understand how thoughtful design choices can make a brand more recognizable and professional. I can apply these skills when creating personal portfolios, business identities, and other branding materials in the future.
</p>
